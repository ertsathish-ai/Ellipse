Construction logic used: At the vertex V, VE = VF. Join D–E and extend. At each axis station A, the perpendicular ordinate intersects DE at A₁. Measure AA₁; with F as centre and AA₁ as radius, cut the vertical through A to obtain the ellipse point. Repeat above and below the axis.
